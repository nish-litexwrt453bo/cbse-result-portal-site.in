Nobel Prize Winners 2026

Celebrating excellence in science, literature, and peace

Medicine

Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for their groundbreaking work in optogenetics, enabling precise control of neurons with light.

Physics

Francis Halzen was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics 2026 for his decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin.

Chemistry

Henri B. Kagan and Kenso Soai for discovering non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis.

Literature

Canadian author Anne Carson for her profound contributions to modern literature.

Peace

Navi Pillay for her lifelong efforts to promote peace, human rights, and international law.
