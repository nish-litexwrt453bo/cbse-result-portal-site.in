Naruto Uzumaki

Naruto Uzumaki is the protagonist of the Naruto series. He dreams of becoming Hokage, the leader of his village. Known for his cheerful personality and determination, Naruto harnesses the power of the Nine-Tails fox spirit within him.

Son Goku

Son Goku from Dragon Ball is a Saiyan warrior famous for his strength, cheerful nature, and love for fighting strong opponents. He is the protector of Earth and has achieved many powerful transformations like Super Saiyan and Ultra Instinct.

Monkey D. Luffy

Luffy is the captain of the Straw Hat Pirates in One Piece. With his Gum-Gum powers, he stretches his body like rubber. His ultimate goal is to find the One Piece and become the Pirate King.

Tanjiro Kamado

Tanjiro Kamado from Demon Slayer is a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is slaughtered. He fights to protect his sister Nezuko, who was turned into a demon, while seeking a cure for her.

Eren Yeager

Eren Yeager from Attack on Titan is a determined fighter who vows to destroy all Titans after witnessing his mother’s death. His journey explores freedom, revenge, and the dark truths of humanity.
